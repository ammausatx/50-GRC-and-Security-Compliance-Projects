# Project 17: ISO 27001 Internal Audit and Evidence Pack

**Level:** Intermediate | **Track:** GRC & Security Compliance

## Objective
Implement a practical iso 27001 internal audit and evidence pack exercise for a fictional organization, producing a traceable readiness assessment and prioritized improvement plan.

## Scope and authorization
Define the legal entity, business services, systems, data, locations, third parties, assessment boundary, exclusions, and time period. Use fictional and synthetic evidence only. Confirm permission before examining any live environment. This is educational and not legal advice, certification, or an audit opinion.

## Prerequisites
- Official current framework publication and applicable interpretive guidance
- Documented scope, asset/service inventory, and named owners
- Spreadsheet or GRC platform, evidence repository, and diagramming tool
- Synthetic policies, control records, tickets, and configuration exports

## Lab setup
1. Create folders for charter, scope, requirements, controls, evidence, findings, remediation, and reporting.
2. Record framework edition, criteria references, assessment date, and assessment type.
3. Create linked registers with unique IDs and controlled status values.
4. Define sampling approach, test steps, evidence quality rules, and reviewer independence.
5. Agree on risk scoring, exception approval, escalation, and evidence retention.

## Walkthrough
### Step 1 — Scope and applicability
Document organizational context, service boundaries, systems, interfaces, locations, suppliers, data flows, and exclusions. Explain why each item is in or out of scope and obtain approval.

### Step 2 — Requirements interpretation
Break the framework into testable statements. Record exact reference, applicability, implementation expectation, accountable owner, design evidence, operating evidence, and test method. Do not rely on an unofficial crosswalk as the authoritative requirement.

### Step 3 — Control design review
For each requirement identify the control objective, process, frequency, performer, reviewer, systems, and escalation path. Evaluate whether the design could reasonably meet the intended outcome.

### Step 4 — Operating effectiveness sampling
Select a risk-based sample from a defined population. Record population size, selection rationale, sample IDs, period, procedure, observed result, exceptions, and evidence reference. Distinguish absence of evidence from evidence that a control failed.

### Step 5 — Determine gaps
Classify each requirement as implemented/effective, partially implemented, not implemented, not applicable with rationale, or unable to verify. Document factual condition, criterion, cause, impact, and limitations.

### Step 6 — Risk and remediation
Rate findings using a defined likelihood/impact model. Assign corrective actions, owners, target dates, interim safeguards, dependencies, and closure evidence. Track accepted risks separately with approver, rationale, expiry, and review date.

### Step 7 — Readiness reporting
Prepare a management report with scope, methodology, maturity/readiness summary, exceptions, evidence limitations, high-priority gaps, and a realistic remediation roadmap. State clearly that readiness work is not an independent certification or attestation.

## Tools and analysis
Use a requirements-to-controls matrix, evidence index, sample tracker, risk register, and corrective-action log. Spreadsheet checks should flag missing owners, duplicate IDs, unsupported conclusions, expired exceptions, and overdue actions. Use official framework materials for authoritative wording and keep licensed standards content within permitted use.

## Expected output
A scoped readiness pack with requirement mapping, control narratives, design and operating tests, evidence references, gap register, risk ratings, approved treatment plan, and management summary.

## Findings and risk assessment
Report control design and operation separately. Consider scope, sample limitations, recurrence, impact, compensating controls, and dependency risk. Avoid claiming compliance based solely on self-attestation or policy existence.

## Framework mapping
This project is centered on the named framework or assessment subject. Where helpful, map related objectives to ISO/IEC 27001:2022, NIST CSF 2.0, NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5, CIS Controls v8, or other applicable criteria. Record exact editions and explain mapping rationale; mappings do not establish equivalence.

## Remediation
Create an action for each confirmed gap with root cause, accountable owner, priority, milestones, due date, interim safeguard, evidence requirement, and independent closure test. Escalate high-risk overdue items.

## Validation criteria
- Approved scope and applicability statement
- Current authoritative criteria and exact references
- Design and operating tests documented separately
- Samples trace to a defined population and period
- Findings trace to evidence and explain limitations
- Risk acceptance is approved and time-bounded
- Closure includes evidence and retesting

## Evidence and reporting checklist
- [ ] Charter, scope, exclusions, and approval
- [ ] Framework edition and criteria register
- [ ] Control owners and control narratives
- [ ] Population, sample method, and test workpapers
- [ ] Evidence index with dates and source
- [ ] Findings, risk ratings, and limitations
- [ ] Corrective actions and accepted-risk approvals
- [ ] Management report and remediation roadmap
